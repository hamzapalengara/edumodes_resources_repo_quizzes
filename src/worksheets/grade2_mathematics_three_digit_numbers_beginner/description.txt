An engaging and interactive worksheet designed to help Grade 2 students (ages 7-8) master three-digit numbers and place values. Features include:

• Interactive place value blocks for hands-on learning
• Visual representation of hundreds, tens, and ones
• Voice guidance and immediate feedback
• Multiple question types: building numbers, identifying place values, and comparing numbers
• Progress tracking and celebratory animations

Learning Objectives:
- Understand place value concepts (hundreds, tens, ones)
- Build and decompose three-digit numbers
- Identify the value of digits based on their position
- Practice number formation and recognition
- Develop mental math skills with three-digit numbers

Perfect for classroom use, homeschooling, or supplementary practice. Includes comprehensive answer key with visual aids and teaching tips for educators and parents.
