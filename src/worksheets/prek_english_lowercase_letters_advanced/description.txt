An interactive letter tracing adventure for preschoolers featuring lowercase letters u through z, each paired with delightful candy and sweet treats! This engaging worksheet includes guided tracing with immediate feedback, delightful animations, speech synthesis for letter pronunciation, and progress tracking. The kid-friendly candy theme makes learning fun with unicorn lollipops, vanilla ice cream, wafer cookies, xtra sweet candy, yogurt parfait, and zebra cake. Features include:

- Step-by-step tracing guidance with animated demonstrations
- Sweet treat associations for each letter
- Immediate visual and audio feedback
- Progress tracking with candy-themed rewards
- Candy-themed tips and tricks for perfect letter formation
- Mobile-friendly design for easy interaction
- Accessibility features for all learners

Perfect for building early writing skills and letter recognition while having fun in a sweet, candy-filled adventure!
