Teaching Tips: Name Recognition Adventure

Getting Started

Before beginning the name recognition activity, ensure:

  • The child is comfortable and ready to learn
  • You have their name spelled correctly as you want them to learn it
  • The device is properly positioned at their eye level
  • The learning environment is free from distractions

Activity Guide

  • Activity 1: Help enter the child's name correctly
  • Activity 2: Guide them in finding letters of their name in the alphabet
  • Activity 3: Assist with the matching game if needed
  • Activity 4: Support them in putting letters in the correct order
  • Activity 5: Watch the letter animations together

Important Reminder

Every child develops at their own pace. Some may need more time with certain activities than others. Always maintain a positive and encouraging environment.

Progress Monitoring

  • Watch how confidently they identify letters
  • Notice if they can match letters consistently
  • Observe their understanding of letter order
  • Monitor their engagement and enjoyment

Extension Activities

  • Practice finding their name letters in books
  • Create a name song together
  • Look for their letters in the environment
  • Draw their letters in sand or with paint

Learning Goals

By completing this worksheet, you should be able to:

  • Recognize your name in print
  • Identify all the letters in your name
  • Match identical letters
  • Put the letters of your name in order
  • Understand how each letter is written
