An engaging and interactive picture-word matching worksheet designed specifically for preschool children (ages 3-4). This worksheet helps young learners develop essential early literacy skills through fun and interactive word-picture associations.

Key Features:
- Interactive picture and word matching with colorful emojis
- Voice guidance and audio feedback for each word
- Immediate visual and auditory feedback
- Progress tracking with celebratory animations
- Simple and intuitive interface for young children

Learning Objectives:
- Recognize and match basic words with corresponding pictures
- Build early reading vocabulary
- Develop word-picture association skills
- Enhance visual recognition abilities
- Practice basic sight words

Perfect for classroom use, homeschooling, or supplementary learning. Includes a comprehensive answer key and teaching tips to support parents and educators in guiding young learners through the activities.
