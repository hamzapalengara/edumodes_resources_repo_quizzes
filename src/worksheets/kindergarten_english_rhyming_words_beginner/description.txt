An engaging and interactive learning experience that helps children identify and match rhyming words through fun activities with colorful visuals and audio feedback. Features include:

• Interactive word matching with emojis and animations
• Voice guidance and audio feedback for better learning
• Visual hints and examples for each word pair
• Immediate feedback and celebrations for correct answers
• Progress tracking and scoring system

Learning Objectives:
- Recognize and identify rhyming word patterns
- Develop phonological awareness
- Build vocabulary through word families
- Improve reading and pronunciation skills
- Practice word recognition and matching

Perfect for classroom use, homeschooling, or supplementary learning activities. Includes comprehensive answer key with example sentences and teaching tips for educators and parents.
